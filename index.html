The next chapter

A relationship changes. Work loses its meaning. Something you built falls apart, or succeeds and leaves you wondering what else there is.

You bring experience, ability, and desires of your own. There are responsibilities to honor too. Second Landings is where I explore how to make room for purpose, connection, adventure, and enjoyment within a real, complicated life.

Who's writing

I'm Dan. I've been married, divorced, and found love again. I dropped out of college and went back during COVID. I've built a career in tech and experienced both success and failure as a real estate investor. I've had plenty of reasons to reconsider what I was building my life around.

I've spent the past decade reinventing and reinvesting in myself. That has included getting into the best shape of my life, climbing Kilimanjaro for my fiftieth birthday, and walking the Camino as an atheist who still felt the power of intention, attention, and something bigger than himself.

Second Landings is where I explore this in public, with people asking their own versions of the question. You're welcome while things are messy, while you're rebuilding, or when life is going well and you wonder what else is possible. We have experience to draw on and more life to discover.
